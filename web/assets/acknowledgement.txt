This app displays SPHEREx data: images from the Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer, a joint project of the Jet Propulsion Laboratory and the California Institute of Technology, funded by NASA. The images were retrieved from the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA), Quick Release 2 (QR2), DOI 10.26131/IRSA652.
