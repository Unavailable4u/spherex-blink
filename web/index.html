How this works

SPHEREx scans the whole sky in 102 infrared colours. Every field is seen on several dates, so a world that moves between visits is easy to catch: this is the blink technique that found Pluto in 1930.

  1. Pick a band and drag the time bar. Stars stay put; movers jump.
  2. Press Space to blink two frames. Use Set B to choose the second frame.
  3. Click a mover to read its position and brightness, then flag it.
  4. Open Context to see this field against optical, infrared and Hubble views, and on the whole sky.
  5. Open Planet X to see what a distant planet would do here, and to measure a mover's rate.
